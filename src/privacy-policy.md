# {{ heading1 }}

At P3+Uplift, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website [www.p3uplift.com](https://p3uplift.com) or use our services.

## Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you contact us or sign up for our services. This may include:

* **Personal Identifiers:** Name, email address, and phone number.
* **Professional Information:** Any other information you provide related to your business or staffing needs.
* **Non-Personal Information:** Usage data and cookies collected to improve our website and customer experience.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

* To provide and manage our services.
* To communicate with you regarding your inquiries or projects.
* To improve our website functionality and user experience.
* To comply with legal obligations.

## Disclosure of Your Information

We value your trust and do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only with:

* **Trusted Service Providers:** Partners who assist us in operating our website and delivering services, all of whom are subject to strict confidentiality agreements.
* **Legal Compliance:** Where required by law or to protect our rights.

## Security of Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## Your Rights

You have the right to control your personal data. Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

* Access the personal information we hold about you.
* Request that we update or correct your information.
* Request the deletion of your personal data.

If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at **careers@p3uplift.com**.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page. Your continued use of our services after any modifications indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
